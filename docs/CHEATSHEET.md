# Makohai: quick cheat sheet

What every control does, by what you want to do. The [manual](MANUAL.md) has every detail.

**Getting around:** one screen, a mixing desk with all six lanes (LEAD, ARP, PAD, BASS, DRUMS, SFX). A group's button
opens it; pressed again (or **PRESETS**), its next setting. **KNOB 1 to 4** set that setting for the four lanes in the
window; **ALGORITHM** marks the lane the buttons and keys act on, and the window follows it. Hold **SEL** for steps of 10.

## Play

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Start | **PLAY**: with an INTRO, the lanes in one by one (SEL + PLAY: all at once) |
| Stop | **PLAY** while playing: the END, lanes fading out one by one (SEL + PLAY: stop now) |
| Tempo | **SELECT** |
| Next style | **GLO** (SONG, LIVE), KNOB 1: queue a style. **SEQ**: LAUNCH, every unlocked lane moves to it (SEL + SEQ: keep your tempo) |
| Blend it in | **GLO** (SONG, LIVE), KNOB 2: **BLEND**, lane by lane from the bottom: drums, bass, pad, lead |
| Build or break down | **GLO** (SONG, LIVE), KNOB 3: **ENERGY**, every lane's density at once |
| One lane alone | **REC** tapped: INTER (two bars, the others back one by one); held: DROP, everything back when you let go |
| A new idea | **LFO**: the marked lane, at once |
| Fade a lane | **ENV**: out over a bar; again: back in. SEL + ENV: LOCK (it keeps its style) |
| Play a lane | **Keys**: the marked lane, live (on PAD a chord in key, on DRUMS the kit). **OCT-** / **OCT+**: the octave |

## The settings groups

| Group | Button | Settings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PLAY | **HOME** | VOLUME, STYLE, SOUND, MELODY, RHYTHM, PITCH, DENSITY, OCTAVE, CHANGE |
| SOUND | **FX** | REVERB, FILTER, RESONANCE, WOBBLE, BRIGHTNESS, DRIVE, CRUSH, VARY, MUTATE |
| TRACKER | **ARP** | PAN, AUTOPAN, PAN RATE, SWING, ECHO, SLIDE, GLIDES, PORTA, VIBRATO, TREMOLO, TREMOR, GATE, RETRIGGER, RATE |
| SONG | **GLO** | LIVE (style, blend, energy, space), KEY (key, scale, chords, bars), ARC (on, build, peak, auto drop), SETS |

## Your own parts

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Edit a lane's part | **EDIT** (again: back); **PRESETS**: NOTES, FX, PART, BAR, RANGE |
| Enter notes | White keys: scale steps from the chord root; black keys: a rest (SEL: a tie) |
| Record live | On EDIT, **REC**: RECORD on. SEL + REC: GRAB what the lane plays into this bar |
| Generate a bar | On EDIT, **LFO**: in a style, or a variation of yours |
| Copy a part | On EDIT, **SEQ**: COPY from the lane set on PART |
| Clear a bar | SEL + **EDIT** |

## Keep and connect

| What | How |
| --- | --- |
| Save everything | **SAVE** (playing: waits until you stop) |
| Sets | **GLO** to SETS: eight sets, load at once while playing |
| Setup | Hold **HOME**: SETUP (LOOK, MIDI channels, SYNC, BACK picture, RESET, SYSTEM, AUDIO, VOICES) |
| MIDI clock | SETUP, SYNC: **CLK OUT**, **CLK IN** (follow a clock), **NOTES IN**, **CC OUT** |
| Pictures | SEL + **HOME**: DEMO, full-screen pictures cut on the beat |
| MIDI out | Lead, pad, bass on channels 1 to 3, the arp on 4, drums on 10 |
